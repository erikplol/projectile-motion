Projectile Motion Theory

Key Equations:

Horizontal velocity: vₓ = v₀ cos θ

Vertical velocity: vᵧ = v₀ sin θ

Maximum height: H = y₀ + (v₀ sin θ)² / (2g)

Time of flight: t = [v₀ sin θ + √((v₀ sin θ)² + 2gy₀)] / g

Range: R = vₓ × t

Position at time t:

x(t) = v₀ cos θ × t

y(t) = y₀ + v₀ sin θ × t - ½gt²

Where:

  • g = 9.81 m/s² (gravitational acceleration)
  • v₀ = initial velocity
  • θ = launch angle
  • y₀ = initial height
